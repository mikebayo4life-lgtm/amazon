Frequently Asked Questions:

1. What's different about Amazon Bazaar?

Amazon Bazaar is a new mobile shopping app that brings ultra-low-priced products into one convenient experience. The app features hundreds of thousands of products across fashion, home, electronics and lifestyle categories. What makes it unique is its blend of value shopping with interactive features like social lucky draws and promotions. The app includes Amazon's trusted shopping experience with 24/7 multilingual support, customer reviews, and free returns within 15 days of receipt.

2. Is my Amazon Bazaar order protected?

Customers will find the Amazon features they know and love, including customer reviews and star ratings, to help them select the right products. All products on Amazon Bazaar undergo Amazon's rigorous compliance checks, so customers can shop with confidence knowing they will receive items that are safe and meet all applicable regulations and Amazon policies.

3. How do I return an item bought on Amazon Bazaar?

Eligible purchases from the Amazon Bazaar app can be returned for free if requested within 15 days of receipt by the customer with convenient and hassle-free returns. Within the app experience customers can click on 'You' tab, click on 'Your orders', click on the order with items they want to return, then click on 'Return.’

4. How do I get free shipping for my orders?

Free shipping applies when orders reach the local minimum purchase amount, with a standard delivery charge for smaller orders.

5. Can I use my Amazon credentials to log in to Bazaar?

Customers can use their existing Amazon credentials or create new accounts to shop on Amazon Bazaar.
